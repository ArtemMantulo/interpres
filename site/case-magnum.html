Magnum Sanur

Digital Twin

Founded in 2019, Magnum Estate is a full-cycle luxury real estate developer in Bali, handling land, architecture, construction and management in-house. Its portfolio spans 8 projects completed and under construction — including the flagship oceanfront Magnum Resort Sanur — and has won the Asia Pacific Property Awards five times.

VR build

A VR build that lets buyers live a day at Magnum Sanur before it is built.

Put on the headset and walk the whole complex at true scale: stroll through the grounds, explore the amenities, the pools and lounge areas, the spa and wellness zones, then step into a residence and out onto its terrace — and feel what it is like to sip a glass of champagne while looking out over the ocean. It turns a floor plan into a memory buyers want to come back to.
